Privacy Policy

Creek Golf Games · Effective October 7, 2026

Creek Golf Games is a scorekeeper for golf side games. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app.

We don't collect your data

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Player names, handicaps, scores, courses you add, and favorite courses are saved only on your device so your round survives closing the app. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app (or clearing this website's data in your browser) removes it.

Saving results

If you tap “Save results,” the app creates a picture of the results and opens the standard iPhone share sheet. Where that picture goes (Photos, Messages, and so on) is your choice, and it's handled by your device, not by us.

Internet use

The iPhone app works without an internet connection and does not contact any server. The website version is hosted by Netlify, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as part of delivering the page; see Netlify's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
